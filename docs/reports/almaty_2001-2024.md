## 5. Hypotheses — proposed, not tested here

These are possible explanations. They are not findings.

- `EV-e417bcaf3e70` (T2M ↔ PRECTOTCORR): Hypothesis (untested here): in the warm season wet months are cloudier and evaporation cools the surface, while in winter mild air masses are also the moist ones, which would reverse the sign. A test: analyse seasons separately and add cloud amount or surface solar radiation.
- `EV-ca4274b02bba` (T2M ↔ RH2M): Hypothesis (untested here): for a fixed amount of water vapour, warmer air is further from saturation, so relative humidity is lower in warmer-than-usual months. A test: repeat with specific humidity (QV2M), which should co-vary with temperature far less if this is the reason.
- `EV-d0c006b3e3b8` (PRECTOTCORR ↔ RH2M): Hypothesis (untested here): wetter-than-usual months bring moister air and wetter soil, and both raise near-surface humidity; humidity staying high in the following month would fit soil-moisture memory. A test: add surface soil wetness (GWETTOP) and check whether it carries the one-month lag.

## 7. Limitations

- One MERRA-2 grid cell (about 0.5 x 0.625 degrees) at the cell's mean elevation: it describes the cell, not a station or a city.
- MERRA-2 is a model reanalysis. Changes in the observations it assimilates can create steps that look like trends; no homogeneity test is applied here.
- Only monotonic (Theil-Sen / Mann-Kendall) and linear trends are tested.
- Correlations describe co-variation of monthly anomalies. They do not identify causes, and a lead or lag is a timing statistic only.
- Results hold for the stated period; other periods can and do give different answers.
