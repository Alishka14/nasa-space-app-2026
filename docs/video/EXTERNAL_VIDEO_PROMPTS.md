# External video generation prompts

> Generated by `scripts/build_video_docs.py` from `intro-scripts.json`. For tools such as Gemini (Veo) or other text-to-video generators. **No video was generated for this repository.**

## Rules that apply to every variant

1. **The generator makes pictures only.** Every word and every number is added in post-production from the on-screen text in the script files (`HERO_EN.md` …). Generators misspell text and invent digits; a wrong correlation on screen would defeat the purpose of the project.
2. **Numbers come from `facts.json` only:** p_rh_raw = −0.285, p_rh_cycles = −0.775, p_rh_deseason = +0.593, t_rh_raw = −0.945, t_rh_deseason = −0.316.
3. **Charts are not generated.** For the correlation and time-series shots, capture the real in-app frames (`?capture&variant=…&lang=…`, see `docs/video/VIDEO_MASTER_PLAN.md`) and use the generated footage only as background plates.
4. **Disclosure.** The NASA Space Apps rules require AI-generated images and video to carry a visible watermark and to be declared in the *Use of Super Intelligence* field.
5. **Length.** The submission demo must be 30 seconds or less. Only the Teaser (and a trimmed Explainer) fits; the Hero film is for presentations.

## Global style prompt

```text
Cinematic scientific visualisation, NASA mission-control mood. Photoreal Earth from space against a deep black sky with sparse sharp stars; day side lit from the left, night side with warm city lights, thin blue atmospheric rim. Cool cyan data overlays (thin coastlines, faint latitude-longitude grid). Slow, steady camera moves with eased starts and stops; no shake, no lens flares, no fast cuts. Colour: near-black navy background, white and cyan accents, blue for negative values, red for positive values. 16:9, 24 fps.
```

## Negative prompt / constraints

```text
No text, numbers, letters, logos or flags rendered by the generator (all text is added in post); no NASA logo or insignia; no people or faces; no fire, storms, disasters or apocalyptic imagery; no glowing 'AI brain' imagery; no cartoon style; no invented charts; no fictional satellites with labels; no cities zoomed to street level; no changes to the shape of continents.
```
