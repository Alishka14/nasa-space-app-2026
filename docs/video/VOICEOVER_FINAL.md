# Final voice-over scripts — English, Русский, Қазақша

Generated by `scripts/build_voiceover.py` from `frontend/src/onboarding/content/intro-scripts.json` and `facts.json`; edit the scripts there, not here. The narration is word for word the subtitle text of the films, so sound and picture never disagree.

**Status.** Voiced with synthetic speech (ElevenLabs, model `eleven_v4`), approved by the team: English in the library voice *Marcus K — Calm Documentary Narrator*, Russian and Kazakh in the designed voice «Иван» from the team's Pavlodar Pulse project. Neither imitates a real narrator or actor, and every voiced file is labelled "AI voice (ElevenLabs)" in the picture and in its metadata. The voiced films are `docs/video/renders/voiced/*_voice.mp4` (placement and slow-downs in `VOICE_TIMING.json`, made by `scripts/build_voiced_films.py`); the silent films are unchanged. Not voiced: Kazakh *Explainer* (lines e3, e5, e7) and Kazakh *Short* (t2, t4) — the account's ElevenLabs credits ran out before these five lines; those two films stay silent with subtitles. Every clip was transcribed with Whisper (local, `small`) and compared with its line: all English and Russian clips match apart from digits written as words; Whisper's Kazakh is too weak for word-level checking, but each Kazakh clip matches its own line phonetically and in its numbers.

**How to record.** One take per scene, starting at the scene's start time; leave the scene's last 0.3 s silent. Read numbers in full exactly as below — they must match the screen. Planning pace: English 2.5, Russian 2.2, Kazakh 2.0 words per second; *fit* compares the spoken length at that pace with the scene.
